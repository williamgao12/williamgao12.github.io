Phone-Detection Deterrent Device

A Raspberry Pi device that spots phone use with YOLO object detection and drops a marble as a deterrent.

What I did

  • Designed the marble drop chute mechanism and camera stand.
  • Wired the system.
  • Contributed to the detection code.

How it works

The system uses a camera and a Raspberry Pi to detect when someone is holding a phone. It also tracks the angles of the user's elbows and the position of their head. We set it up so that when someone is hunched over looking at their phone, the marbles drop. My favorite part of this project was coming up with the concept. It was a fun idea, and seeing it come together was exciting.

What went wrong and how I fixed it

The biggest challenge I ran into was the gap between how parts look in the CAD assembly and how they actually come out of the 3D printer. Parts that fit together on screen didn't always fit in real life, so I added about 0.1 mm of clearance between mating parts to get them to fit. I also had to print the design in multiple pieces, so I designed them to fit together without any extra parts. The time pressure was tough, but it was rewarding to see how much we could build in about a day.
