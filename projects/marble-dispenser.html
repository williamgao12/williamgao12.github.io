Marble Dispensing Mechanism

A motor-driven marble dispenser designed and built by a 4-person team.

  • SolidWorks
  • 3D printing
  • DC motors
  • Simulation
WhenFall 2024, Carnegie Mellon University
My roleDesign, fabrication, simulation
ToolsSolidWorks, ANSYS Discovery, laser cutter, 3D printer, DC motor

What I did

  • Designed and fabricated a fully functional dispenser with laser-cut wood, 3D-printed parts, and a DC motor drive.
  • Ran stress and motion simulations to validate stability and tune part geometry for smooth, consistent dispensing.

How it works

I worked with three teammates on this project. The dispenser works by loading multiple marbles into a hopper. A DC motor turns a wheel with a marble compartment, which picks up one marble at a time and dispenses it at regular intervals. The wheel is 3D printed, the wooden housing was cut by hand with a jigsaw, and the ramp the marbles roll out on was laser cut.

What went wrong and how I fixed it

One problem with this project was that our initial design was more complicated than it needed to be. It used about three gears, but each gear meant drilling more holes in the back panel and added more room for error, so I simplified it down to one larger motor. We also had to work under time pressure, but we managed by communicating when each of us was free and deliberately splitting the work based on our strengths.
